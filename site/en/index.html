Install

Three steps to your first clip

Download the installer

The file ClipSearchSetup.exe is under a megabyte: it downloads the rest by itself. Don’t want to install? Take the ZIP archive and unpack it anywhere.

Run it

The installer asks for a folder and creates shortcuts. No administrator rights needed; WebView2 already comes with Windows 10 and 11.

If a blue “Windows protected your PC” window appears, click More info, then Run anyway. That’s how Windows treats apps without a paid digital signature.

Search for clips

Enter a streamer, choose a period and press “Search”. “Twitch connected” in the bottom left means everything works.

Download the DMG

The file ClipSearch-mac.dmg works on any Mac, both Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) and Intel. macOS 11 or later is required.

Move it to Applications

Open the DMG, drag ClipSearch to the Applications folder and launch it from there.

On first launch macOS will say it can’t verify the developer. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once: that’s how macOS treats apps without a paid Apple signature.

FAQ

In short

Is it free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no ads.

Do I need a Twitch account?

No. You don’t need to sign in to Twitch or enter keys: the app searches public clips.

Where are clips saved?

To the folder you choose in “Downloads”. You can open or change it right from the app.

Which devices does ClipSearch run on?

The app is available for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and for macOS 11 or later — on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) and on Intel Macs. There's no app for phones or tablets, but this site works on any device: you can search clips, watch them and download them one by one, and download broadcast videos too.

How do I update?

No need to download anything again: when a new version comes out, the app offers to update. You can check manually with “Check for updates” in the bottom left.

Is it safe?

The source code is open on GitHub, and the installer and archive are built there automatically. The app never asks for passwords and doesn’t touch your Twitch account.
